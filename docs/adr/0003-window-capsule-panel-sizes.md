# Two rest window sizes, fixed open window

Status: accepted; amended 2026-10-28 by ADR-0009, by the two-width pass, by the 100/136 music pass, and by the 100-rest pass. **Superseded in part 2026-11-11:** the sizes below are historical. Current geometry lives in DESIGN.md §3 and §5: the resting capsule hugs its content (idle 78, files 80–88, music 69–108, timer 122, done 108, drag 88, error 127, quiet 26×26) and music expands to 126×30 on hover inside the same 136×42 window; the open window is 452×316.

The 800×60 window stretched the capsule across the screen, and the 360×460 panel left dead space. The rest window is 144×44 for every state. The capsule rests at 100×32 in every state; music, timer, and done grow to 136×32 on hover or focus over 120ms, drag is 136×32, and idle and files stay 100. The pill stays bottom-center and grows both ways inside the fixed window, so no Win32 resize happens during pointer movement. Every state rests small because the resting pill only hints at one fact; the title, artist, details, and full controls live in the panel. A click opens a 452×332 window that contains the 420×300 panel, leaving a 16px transparent margin for the panel shadow. Radii are 6 micro, 8 control, 12 card/art, 16 panel, and 999 capsule/switch. The panel body padding is 8 and there is no footer. The music art is a 148×148 square.
