# Capsule interaction: filled transport, title/transport swap, control focus

Status: accepted; amended 2026-10-28 by ADR-0009, which replaced hover growth, the 40px title slot, and the mascot pop, by the 100/136 music pass, and by the 100-rest pass; amended 2026-10-29 by ADR-0010, which replaced the paused play glyph with artwork and an amber dot.

Filled media glyphs (previous, play/pause, next) read clearly at 16px where outline icons looked like wireframes. Every state rests at 100×32; music, timer, and done grow to 136×32 on hover or focus over 120ms. At idle the label is muted, the star sits at 70%, and both return to full on hover while the border strengthens; the pill does not grow. On music hover or keyboard focus, the star and title fade out over 120ms and three 28px transports fade in across the full pill, keeping 8px edge padding. Timer keeps its digits and reveals a 28px cancel; done replaces its check with `Done` and a 28px dismiss; neither grows when there is nothing to reveal. Keyboard focus uses a 1px neutral inset ring on the focused control; the capsule itself has no ring and no glow. The mascot is static. A paused capsule keeps a 12px play glyph outside the slot, leaving 30px for the muted title at rest, making the state readable without hovering. The app and preview share `pill.css`.
