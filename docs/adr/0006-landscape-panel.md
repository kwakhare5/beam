# Landscape panel with square art

Status: accepted; amended 2026-10-28 by ADR-0009, which changed the open window to 452×332 and made the music art square, and by the flat-surface pass, which fixed the calendar at six rows.

The 360×340 panel was nearly square and the full-bleed art left a dead bottom area. The panel is 420×300 inside a 452×332 open window with a 16px shadow margin, so the shadow has room and the transparent margin still counts as an outside click. The music tab uses a 148×148 square art tile on the left and a right column with title, artist, a read-only position bar, and the controls. The panel body is 258px after the 40px tab bar and 2px border; the settings tab fits five 44px rows by using dividers instead of a row gap, the file list shows five rows and scrolls beyond that, and the calendar always draws six week rows padded with adjacent-month days so the grid never jumps between months. There is no footer. The panel expands downward when the capsule is in the upper half of the display and upward when it is in the lower half.
