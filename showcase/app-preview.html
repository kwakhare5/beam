2 · Capsule

One 136×42 window. Every rest state hugs its content with equal 8px padding, and the star + label center as one unit. Clicking anywhere on the rest pill opens the panel. Idle hugs its label at 78×30 and quiets to a 26×26 star after 10s; a cursor within 37px of the centre or a hover brings the label back, and it quiets again 2s after the cursor leaves. Music and paused hug the title up to 108×30; on hover only, the pill expands once to 126×30 and shows prev / play / next / panel in a single 24px row. The expansion snaps so no target moves. Timer and done always show their 28px ×. Music swaps the star for the album thumbnail; paused adds an amber dot. A long title scrolls to its end and pauses on hover.

4 · Rules

The values this file demonstrates. Shadcn-style variables; vanilla TypeScript; Lucide.

Sizes

  • Capsule 26×26 quiet · rest states hug their content · music hover 126 · 4 × 24px controls with 4px gaps · timer and done always show a 28px ×
  • Window 136×42 for every state
  • Near wake 37px from the centre (~24px off the nub); quiet after 10s; returns 2s after the cursor leaves
  • Panel 420×284
  • Open window 452×316 · 16px shadow margin
  • Radii 6 / 8 / 12 / 16; hit 24 / 28 / 32

Type & icons

  • Inter variable, vendored
  • Scale 11 / 12 / 13 / 15 / 40
  • Capsule 12 / 600; panel title 15 / 600
  • Timer 15 / 40 / 600; tabular
  • Icons 20 / 16 / 14 / 12

States

  • Capsule quiet · idle · playing · paused · timer · done · files · drag · error
  • Interaction rest · hover · focus · pressed · disabled
  • Panel empty · loading · success
  • Capsule 1px · panel 2px focus
  • Tab bar four equal labeled tabs + a 28px icon-only Settings button; selected = gold + 2px underline, no fill
  • Dots green playing · amber paused · red missing
  • Capsule art 20px thumbnail, star fallback
  • One fill level: no box inside a box
  • Shell solid · no blur · no rim · no glow
  • No capsule glow

Motion

  • Hover color 120ms
  • Music hover expansion snap, no width animation
  • Press 100ms
  • Title marquee 1.4s delay, pauses on hover
  • Beam always animates (Windows animations off)
  • No done glow
